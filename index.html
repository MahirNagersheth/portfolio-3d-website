AI-Powered Agentic Portfolio Management System

A 13-agent LangGraph pipeline that analyzes stocks across fundamentals, technicals, sentiment, and news, runs an adversarial bull/bear debate, applies a three-perspective risk team with a hard 20% concentration cap, and pauses for human-in-the-loop approval before paper-executing trades. Featured · Deep Generative Models

Time-Adaptive Masked DiT × Optimal-Transport Flow

A class-conditional latent Diffusion Transformer for ImageNet-100 image generation that combines two training-time accelerators: minibatch optimal-transport coupling for flow matching, and a time-adaptive token-masking schedule on a DiT-XL backbone. A 2×2 factorial study at fixed scale shows the two interventions interact non-additively — alone neither helps, but together they beat the additive baseline by 1.9 FID points under a Heun-40 / CFG-1.75 5k-sample protocol. Built in PyTorch on VAE-compressed latents.
